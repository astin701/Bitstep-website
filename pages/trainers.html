OUR PROGRAMS

Training Programs

Bitstep Academy offers practical, engaging, and career-oriented training programs that help learners develop knowledge, confidence, and real-world skills.

Reading with Phonics

Improve reading, pronunciation, vocabulary, and fluency through fun phonics-based learning activities.

Grammar Mastery

Build a strong foundation in English grammar for better writing and confident communication.

Spoken English

Enhance speaking confidence, pronunciation, vocabulary, and conversational English skills.

Handwriting

Develop neat handwriting through guided practice, proper techniques, and regular exercises.

IELTS / TOEFL / Cambridge

Prepare for international English examinations with expert coaching and mock tests.

Olympiad Coaching

Strengthen logical thinking and problem-solving skills through structured Olympiad preparation.

Spell Bee Coaching

Improve vocabulary, spelling accuracy, pronunciation, and language confidence.

Placement Training

Prepare for interviews, aptitude tests, resume writing, and group discussions.

Soft Skills Training

Develop communication, teamwork, leadership, and professional workplace skills.

Communication Skills

Learn public speaking, presentations, and effective interpersonal communication.

Faculty Enhancement

Professional development programs for educators using modern teaching strategies.

NLP & Psychology Workshops

Improve confidence, emotional intelligence, motivation, and personal growth.
